# Log 0002 — M1: ltl semantics over traces

Date: 2026-07-01 (same session as 0001). Objective: `Prop'`, `sat`,
`holds` for the CIRCT ltl fragment, with proved lemmas and executable
sanity tests. (M1's other deliverables — `Design`/`Trace`/`simulate` and
their lemmas — landed in M0.)

## What was tried / decided

### Sequence semantics: length-indexed tight matching

The load-bearing design choice. `SMatch t s i len` = "s matches the
interval of `len` ticks starting at tick i". Rationale:

- `ltl.concat` is `##0` *fusion* (end of LHS = start of RHS, same tick),
  so plain pointwise satisfaction can't express it; you need match
  endpoints. With lengths: `len + 1 = la + lb` and RHS starts at
  `i + la - 1`. Sanity theorem `smatch_concat_atom`: fusion of two
  booleans is their conjunction — exactly the identity stated in the
  CIRCT LTL doc.
- `len = 0` (empty match) exists only via `rep _ 0 _`, and `concat`
  requires both operands non-empty (IEEE's "fusion with empty fails").
  Consequence recorded as decision #7: CIRCT desugars `a ##1 b` to
  `concat(a, delay(b,1,0))`, so an empty-matchable `a` behaves
  differently than IEEE's `(empty ##n s) ≡ ##(n-1) s` rule would
  suggest. Which behavior circt-bmc implements is an M3 differential
  question; our semantics is the honest, documented reading of the
  CIRCT ops as specified.
- Repetition composes matches back-to-back (`##1` separation, so total
  length is the sum), via `RepMatch` with a repetition count; windows
  reuse CIRCT's `(base, optional length)` encoding for both delay and
  repeat, `none` = unbounded-but-finite.

### Property semantics

`sat` at a tick: sequences-as-properties are *strong* match existence
(decision #8 — weak/strong only diverge for unbounded sequences on our
infinite traces; M3's BMC cross-check will pin down what circt-bmc does
for `assert property` of an unbounded sequence). `until'` weak and
non-overlapping, `eventually` strong (per CIRCT docs, rule 3). `imp` is
overlapping `|->`: consequent evaluated at the match's *end* tick
(`i + len - 1`), antecedent matches of length 0 do not trigger
(decision #7). `always` added for SVA's implicit per-tick assertion
quantification (decision #9): `holds D p = ∀ valid t, sat t p 0`, and
the importer will wrap `assert property` bodies in `always`.

### Deferred (tracked)

`ltl.goto_repeat`, `ltl.non_consecutive_repeat` (not needed until a
design uses `[->]`/`[=]`), `ltl.clock` (single-clock designs: no-op;
importer will verify the clock is the design's clock and discharge).

### Proofs and tests

- 19 new lemmas proved, including `holds_and_iff` (the M1 named target),
  `sat_until_ff_iff` (weak-until against never-true is `always` — the
  weakness witness), `sat_until_of_q_now` (non-overlap witness),
  `smatch_concat_atom` (fusion sanity), always/eventually distribution
  and idempotence, `smatch_delay_zero_iff` (`##[0:0]` is identity).
  Axiom gate: 251 declarations, all standard (+1 reported bv_decide
  reflection from M0's smoke test).
- `smatchB`: bounded executable twin of `SMatch` on finite traces (all
  quantifiers become bounded searches; a match of length `len` bounds
  component lengths by `len` and repetition counts by `n + len`). Test
  oracle and future `Disproven`-replay engine; *never* citable as proof
  until the M3 agreement lemmas land — this is deliberate scope control,
  logged here per the honesty rules.
- 14 LSpec cases over an input-probe design: fusion, `##1`, bounded and
  unbounded delays, `[*2:3]`, `[*0]` empty match, intersect vs or.

### Friction log

- `lake` only builds exe-imported modules that belong to a declared
  target: `Tests/Ltl.lean` needed a `lean_lib TestSuites` entry.
- `le_refl` is Mathlib-ese; core is `Nat.le_refl`. Preprocessing with
  `omega` for the window arithmetic avoids fragile `Nat.add` defeq
  reasoning.
